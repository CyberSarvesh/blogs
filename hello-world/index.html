Every developer blog starts with a post about starting a blog. This is that post.

What goes here

Mostly backend things I’ve built, broken, or finally understood:

  • Queues and workers. Why the slow part of a request shouldn’t be in the request.
  • Databases. Postgres, indexes, and why SELECT * keeps showing up in my nightmares.
  • Systems programming. I’m learning C and Rust the slow way, and I’ll write down what I learn as I go.
  • Post-mortems. Mine, of my own projects. Friendly ones.

Why a subdomain?

The portfolio at sarveshmohite.me is the brochure. blog.sarveshmohite.me is the notebook. Both are built from one Astro codebase, so the notebook gets the same theme and command palette (press Ctrl + K).

How to reach me

If something here is wrong, and some of it will be, tell me. The email is at the bottom of every post.
